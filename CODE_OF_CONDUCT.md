# Community conduct

We want project discussions and contributions to remain useful, respectful,
and accessible to people with different backgrounds and experience.

Be constructive when reviewing work. Address code, evidence, and behavior
rather than personal characteristics. Respect privacy, and give people space
to disagree or disengage.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory slurs, doxxing,
unwelcome sexual attention, and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove material that violates these
expectations, and may restrict participation where necessary. Actions should
be proportionate to the behavior, with particular care for the safety and
privacy of the people involved.

To raise a conduct concern, use an available private maintainer contact
listed by the relevant project. If none is listed, use GitHub's platform
reporting tools for abuse; avoid exposing another person's sensitive
information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should instead
follow the project's security policy or private vulnerability reporting.

This document is an account-wide fallback. A repository-specific code of
conduct takes precedence when one exists.
